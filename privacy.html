Privacy policy

Media Ops Uploader is used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API.

What it accesses

After the owner signs in with Google, it can manage the owner's own YouTube channels: upload videos, set titles, descriptions, thumbnails and playlists, and read basic channel information (channel name and ID) to confirm it is working on the right channel.

What it stores

Only a sign-in token for each channel, kept on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sent to any other server, shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising. No data from any other person is collected.

YouTube

Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be withdrawn at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
